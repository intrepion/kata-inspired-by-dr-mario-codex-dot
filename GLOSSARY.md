# Prism Orchard
A night gardener restores five floating orchards by releasing light trapped in crystalline knots.

## Language
**Knot**: A fixed colored crystal that must be cleared to restore an orchard. _Avoid_: Virus, enemy.

**Seed pair**: Two linked colored seeds that fall together. _Avoid_: Capsule, pill.

**Half**: One seed from a pair; it becomes independent when its partner clears.

**Bloom**: A horizontal or vertical run of at least four matching colors, cleared simultaneously.

**Cascade**: Additional blooms caused by falling seeds after a bloom.

**Orchard**: One campaign board with a finite set of knots.

**Restoration**: Clearing every knot in an orchard.
